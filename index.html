Before Downloading an App

Search results may show official services, independent articles, advertisements, and third-party downloads. A familiar logo or HTTPS connection alone does not establish authenticity.

  • Independently verify the publisher and website domain.
  • Avoid unverified APK files and unexpected installation prompts.
  • Check requested permissions and privacy information.
  • Keep your phone and browser updated.
  • Never disclose passwords, one-time codes, or payment credentials to unknown parties.
  • Review local eligibility rules and the service's terms before participating.
